# Motion principles

- **Hook inside 3 s.** The first scene names the viewer's problem.
- **One idea per scene, one recurring element across scenes.** Here that is the tutor. Continuity turns slides into motion design.
- **Reveal as the explanation reaches it.** Don't dump a scene in its first quarter and hold it.
- **Permission vs. precision.** For exploration, "like it's your showreel, go all out". For production, give a timeline in seconds plus explicit bans ("no bounce, no particles, no lens flares").
- **Exact words.** Copy comes from the storyboard verbatim, and so do numbers, which come only from verified facts.
- **Outgoing leaves before incoming arrives.** Never cross-fade two texts in the same place.
- **Timings.** Entrances 0.3–0.5 s with `power2/3.out`. Micro reveals 0.12–0.18 s. Stagger 0.05–0.16 s. Springs with a tiny overshoot at most.
- **Banned by default:** letter-by-letter typewriter, bouncing, gradient text, fake UI, invented data.
- **Readability.** Few words, big type, and text kept out of the platform safe zones (`formats.md`).
- **Revise with timecodes.** "At 00:09:10 hold the mark 12 frames longer" is a usable revision. "Make it better" is not.
